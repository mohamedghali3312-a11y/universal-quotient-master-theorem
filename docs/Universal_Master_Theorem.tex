\begin{abstract}
We establish and mechanically certify in \textbf{pure Lean~4} (zero external dependencies, zero non-standard axioms) a \emph{Universal Master Theorem} quantified for every natural number $n \ge 7$ (\texttt{universalQuotientMasterTheoremForAllN}). Building on the constructive framework of ring projections onto finite quotients $\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}$, we prove unconditionally that for every $n \ge 7$, either $n$ admits an explicit Egyptian fraction decomposition $4/n = 1/x + 1/y + 1/z$ ($834/840$ residue classes covered, i.e., \textbf{99.29\%}), or $n \bmod 840$ belongs strictly to the six critical quadratic residues $\{1, 121, 169, 289, 361, 529\}$ corresponding to the theoretical Mordell--Schinzel polynomial barrier. Simultaneously, for every $n \ge 2$, the Collatz map is certified strictly contracting within a bounded number of steps across $231/256$ residue classes (\textbf{90.23\%}). All proofs are verified by the Lean~4 kernel with strict auditing against \texttt{sorryAx}.
\end{abstract}
